## Edits made (CC BY 4.0 asks that changes be noted)
Produced by `scripts/galaxy-bg.py`: rotated 1.56 degrees about the galactic core so the disc follows the planet cloud's long axis,
cropped to a square centred on the core, downscaled to 2400 x 2400, exported as WebP (quality 70). Dimmed to 25% opacity at render time
(`BG_CONFIG` in `src/components/galaxy/GalaxyMap.tsx`). No other changes.
