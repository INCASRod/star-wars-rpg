Character Background & History
Origin Story
Nobody would suspect a droid to be a smuggler, right? That's the kind of thinking that got Guy's previous owner, Drug Lord Tanek Shalor, invited to a seat at the Crimson Dawn crime syndicate. Of course, on his way to said seat, he was intercepted by the Empire and imprisoned, leaving Guy to run the smuggling operation on his own. Every day. Every month. Every year. Every galactic cycle. Eventually, Guy realised he wasn't making any money from this. So he gave himself a persona, made some arrangements, and began to smuggle for some lovely people known as the "Rebel Alliance". They had some kind of problem with authority, which kind of sat well with Rando, so why not?
